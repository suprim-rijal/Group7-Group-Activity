1. Our Sprint Goal
Enable customers to view restaurant details, understand their delivery costs upfront, and successfully place a basic cash-on-delivery order.

2. Our selected stories
Item #1: As a customer, I want to see restaurant details so that I can decide where to order. 
New Item (From Card 7): As a customer, I want to review my cart and place my order using 'Cash on Delivery' so that I can successfully request food. 
New Item (From Card 5): As a customer, I want to see the delivery cost before checkout so that I am not surprised by extra fees.

3. Our strongest INVEST decision
Our strongest decision was enforcing the "I" (Independent) principle by untangling the checkout process (Trouble Card 7). By separating the core checkout flow from the complicated credit card payment story, we ensured the team could actually build, test, and deliver a working order system in a single Sprint without getting stuck.

4. Our strongest DEEP decision
Our strongest decision was proving our backlog is "E" (Emergent) by adapting to new research (Trouble Card 5). As soon as we learned users were abandoning carts due to hidden fees, we adjusted our priorities and immediately added the delivery cost feature to Sprint 2 to solve a real user problem.

5. The biggest change we made to the backlog
The biggest change was completely restructuring the checkout and payment flow. We deleted confusing items, combined the core tasks into one testable cash-on-delivery story, and pushed the card payment feature lower down the list. This turned an impossible, tangled mess into a clean, achievable Sprint.
